Independent concept

Macaroon Club

A playful dessert campaign built around colour, texture and oversized type.

Project type
Independent concept
My role
Campaign design · Art direction

01

The challenge

Turn macarons into the heroes of a nostalgic campaign while keeping enough variety for a social feed.

02

The direction

Treat each dessert like a magazine cover star. Shift the scale between quiet product moments and expressive type.

03

The visual system

Pistachio green, cherry red and warm paper texture, with oversized serif headlines and repeated framing.

#D8DEC5#AA302F#F4E8D2

04

Design principles

  1. A flavour-led palette
  2. Dessert as the focal point
  3. Variation through scale

05

The finished work

Nine finished campaign posts. The series explores a visual direction for food promotion; packaging is a future application, not a completed deliverable.
