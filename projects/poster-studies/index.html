The challenge

Explore how type and composition can carry the feeling of a poster before the viewer reads every word.

02

The direction

Let each poster keep its own character. Weekend Blooms and Lalagül are separate visual studies, collected here as a compact selection.

03

The visual system

Expressive typography, shifts in scale and deliberate contrast. Each composition is an independent experiment.

04

Design principles

  1. Find the visual focal point
  2. Test the hierarchy
  3. Balance type and image

Two independent finished posters, selected for their different moods.
