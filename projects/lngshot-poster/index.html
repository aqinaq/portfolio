Independent concept · 2026

LNGSHOT — Upcoming Album

A high-energy music poster built from bold type, electric colour and cut-out portraiture.

Project type
Independent concept
My role
Poster design · Art direction
Year
2026

01

The challenge

Turn a dense set of album details into one immediate composition while keeping the artists, title and release date easy to find.

02

The direction

Treat the poster like a collectible music insert: loud, layered and deliberately nostalgic, with the group framed as the central visual event.

03

The visual system

Acid yellow, sky blue and signal red; outlined display type; an oval portrait frame; and compact track-list details inspired by printed album graphics.

#EFF514#A8C7E7#ED2729

04

Design principles

  1. Establish a loud three-colour palette
  2. Build hierarchy around title and portrait
  3. Layer release and track details
  4. Finish with print-inspired texture

05

The finished work

One finished promotional poster with a playful Y2K-inspired music aesthetic. An independent concept; not commissioned by or affiliated with LNGSHOT or Jay Park.
