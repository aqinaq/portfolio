Independent concept

Claude Magazine

An editorial concept that makes a technology topic feel human and curious.

Project type
Independent concept
My role
Editorial design · Art direction

01

The challenge

Explore a warmer visual language for an AI topic, away from the usual technology-advertising imagery.

02

The direction

Start with questions. Conversational copy, modular frames and tactile texture make room for curiosity.

03

The visual system

Cream, coral and blue; flexible frames; conversational headlines and an editorial mix of image and type.

04

Design principles

  1. Questions as the starting point
  2. A flexible frame system
  3. Texture for a human touch

05

The finished work

Eight connected editorial posts. Independent concept work; not commissioned by or affiliated with Anthropic.
