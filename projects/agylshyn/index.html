An interactive English-learning platform for Kazakh-speaking learners.

Project type

My role

01

The challenge

Make independent English practice easier to navigate for Kazakh-speaking learners, with a clear route from choosing material to working through exercises.

02

The direction

Build around the study session. Learning materials, exercises and feedback share one focused workspace, supported by Kazakh and English interface options.

03

The visual system

A structured library leads into a focused exercise workspace. Clear navigation, progress cues and bilingual labels help learners stay oriented.

A live bilingual learning platform with exercises, mistake review, progress views and saved-word practice.
