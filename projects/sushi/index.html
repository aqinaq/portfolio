Independent concept

Sushi Social Club

A nine-post food campaign with the attitude of a fashion editorial.

Project type
Independent concept
My role
Art direction · Social campaign

01

The challenge

Give an imaginary sushi club a recognisable presence across a social feed. The challenge: connect nine different compositions without making every post look the same.

02

The direction

Appetite meets attitude. Close food crops and oversized type give the product the presence of an editorial cover.

03

The visual system

Deep red, cream and black; condensed headlines; alternating product-led and type-led layouts.

#671A20#F4E7CE#242323

04

Design principles

  1. One recognisable palette
  2. Product and type in dialogue
  3. A repeatable nine-post rhythm

05

The finished work

Nine finished social designs connected by colour, scale and composition. An independent concept campaign.
