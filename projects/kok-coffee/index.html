Kök Coffee

An early coffee-brand concept exploring a distinct visual direction.

Project type
Independent concept
My role
Brand exploration · Graphic design

01

The challenge

Explore how a coffee concept can express its own character in a single graphic composition.

02

The direction

Use the relationship between the name, type and colour to establish an initial visual direction.

03

The visual system

A single concept artwork. This is an initial brand exploration, with room to develop a fuller identity system in a future project.

04

Design principles

  1. Name and visual character
  2. Type and colour relationships
  3. One finished composition

05

The finished work

One completed brand-concept graphic. Presented as an exploration rather than a complete identity or client commission.
