Self-initiated product · 2026

Mountain

A calm reading space for English books, vocabulary and reading progress.

Project type
Self-initiated product
My role
Product design · AI-assisted development
Year
2026

01

The challenge

Reading in a second language often means leaving the page to look up a word. Mountain brings reading and language support into one place.

02

The direction

Keep the book at the centre. A quiet, editorial interface gives navigation, vocabulary and progress their own place without overwhelming the reading experience.

03

The visual system

Serif headings paired with a simple sans-serif interface. Paper-like surfaces, forest-green accents, thin rules and generous spacing create a restrained reading environment.

#F3F7F3#35614B#242B26

Built with: Next.js · React · Responsive web
Catalogue: public-domain books from Project Gutenberg.

04

Product flow

  1. Discover — browse the catalogue
  2. Save — build a personal library
  3. Read — open a book or import a file
  4. Learn — collect vocabulary
  5. Track — revisit reading progress

05

Selected screens

A working web reading product with a catalogue, personal library, vocabulary and progress views, plus light and dark themes.

06

The result

A working web reading product with a catalogue, personal library, vocabulary and progress views, plus light and dark themes.
